# Nimu, My Best Friend

## About This

A nostalgic letter in motion celebrating a friendship that began in high school in 2022 and became something deeper—the kind of bond where a best friend becomes family.

## The Story

We met in 2022 and somehow the world made room for a sister disguised as a best friend. This is the memory lane of our friendship, documented in cinematic detail.
